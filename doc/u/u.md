# µ
## [[DSL]] for [[embedded]] development

- [[programming language]] specially designed for [[embedded]] systems development
- **targets [[MCU]] first**: runs on smallest devices with 20K+ SRAM
- cross-node binary portability:
	- compiles into FORTH-like stack-based ISA for tight [[bytecode]]
	- runs on top of tiny minimal [[VM]] can be ported in a week to any hw/arch
